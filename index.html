Approach

Most school software is too expensive, or too generic.

So I make my own: workbooks that generate admit cards, web apps for daily records, and now hardware that sits on the wall and does the job by itself.

Everything here was built from scratch, usually for close to nothing.

  • Based in Shillong, Meghalaya
  • Teaches Python, mathematics and physics
  • Works as a data analyst
  • Civil engineer: DPRs, BOQs and cost estimates
  • Builds with ESP32 boards, Android and the web
  • Handles school admin: registers, question papers, admit cards
  • Writes songs on the side
  • Supports FC Barcelona
